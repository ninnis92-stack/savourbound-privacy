# SavourBound Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

SavourBound is developed by Naheem Innis. This policy explains what information SavourBound uses and what happens when you choose to share content.

## Your cookbooks and photos

Cookbooks, recipes, notes, meal plans, shopping lists, and photos are stored on your device. When iCloud syncing is available and enabled for SavourBound, your personal library syncs through your private iCloud account. The developer does not operate or have access to that private library.

Photos selected from your library or captured with the camera stay in your personal library unless you choose to publish a recipe or cookbook to Community. Recipe-card scanning and ingredient recognition are performed on your device.

## Community sharing

Community is optional. When you publish, the content you choose to include—such as recipe text, cookbook title and note, selected photos or cover art, and your optional cook name—is stored in SavourBound's public CloudKit database and can be viewed by other SavourBound users. Apple CloudKit associates published records with the iCloud account that created them. Your Apple Account email address is not displayed in the app, but CloudKit account metadata is used to manage ownership and removal of your posts.

You can remove your published recipes and cookbooks from Settings & Help → Community → Remove everything I've shared. Removing a post does not remove copies that other people already saved to their own libraries.

## Reports and blocking

When you report Community content, SavourBound stores the selected reason, the reported item's identifier, and the report date in CloudKit. CloudKit also associates the report record with the iCloud account that submitted it. Reports are used to investigate misuse. Reports do not expire automatically; to request removal of report information, email the contact below.

Reported content is hidden from your Community view after a report is successfully submitted. A post is hidden from everyone after reports from three distinct iCloud accounts, pending review. Blocking a cook is saved on your device and hides that cook's posts from your Community view on that device; it does not prevent that cook from using Community or hide their posts for other users.

## Speech

When you start voice dictation or cooking voice control, SavourBound listens while its listening indicator is active. Speech recognition runs on-device when supported. Otherwise, Apple may process audio using its speech-recognition service under Apple's privacy terms. SavourBound does not store audio recordings.

## Purchases

SavourBound Plus purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. SavourBound receives purchase entitlement information needed to unlock Plus; the developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles subscription billing, renewal, cancellation, and refunds.

## Data use and retention

SavourBound does not use advertising, cross-app tracking, or analytics SDKs. The app uses Apple's iCloud/CloudKit services for personal syncing and optional public Community sharing, and Apple's speech-recognition service when on-device recognition is unavailable.

Your personal library remains on your device and in your private iCloud until you delete it or disable/remove SavourBound's iCloud data. Community posts remain public until you remove them or they are removed for violating the Community rules. Report records have no automatic expiry and are retained for moderation unless removed after a request.
