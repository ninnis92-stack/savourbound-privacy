# SavourBound Community rules

Community lets SavourBound cooks share cookbooks and recipes with each other. Sharing is optional, and nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. These rules keep it a friendly place to cook.

SavourBound is made by Naheem Innis, an independent developer (“he,” “him,” or “his”).

## What you can share
- Recipes and cookbooks you wrote, or have the right to share.
- Photos you took of food you made.

## What you can't share
- Offensive, hateful, sexual or violent content, harassment, or threats.
- Spam or advertising.
- Text or photos copied from cookbooks, websites or other people without permission. Recipes imported from a website stay private and can't be shared.
- Unsafe food advice.
- Other people's personal information.

## Reporting and blocking
- Every shared recipe and cookbook has a **Report** option. Pick a reason and send it. Other users never see who reported something.
- You can **Block** a cook. Their posts are hidden from your Community view on your device.
- Something you report is hidden for you straight away. If three different iCloud accounts report the same item, it is hidden from everyone while it waits for review.

## What happens to reports
- Reports are reviewed by the developer, not only by software.
- He aims to review safety concerns (threats, self-harm, content involving children, sexual content) the same day, and everything else within two days.
- A post that breaks the rules is removed. A cook who keeps breaking the rules can lose the ability to share. They keep full use of the rest of the app and their own cookbooks.
- A basic word check runs when you share. It can't catch everything, so reports matter.

## Appeals
If your post was removed or your sharing was turned off and you think that was a mistake, email **dbzee15@yahoo.com** within 14 days. Say what you shared and why you think it follows the rules. The decision will be looked at again, and you will get an answer by email.

## Contact
Questions, safety concerns, or something to report outside the app: **dbzee15@yahoo.com**.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
