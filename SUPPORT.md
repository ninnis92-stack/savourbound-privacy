# SavourBound Support

SavourBound is a recipe app that keeps your cooking in beautiful cookbooks you can flip through like paper.

## Get help

Email **dbzee15@yahoo.com** with your question, the device you're using and what happened. You'll usually hear back within two working days.

## Common questions

**My cookbooks aren't showing on my other device.**
Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is on for SavourBound: open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then SavourBound.

**How do I restore SavourBound Plus?**
In SavourBound, open Settings & help and tap Restore Purchases. Plus is tied to the Apple Account that bought it.

**How do I cancel SavourBound Plus?**
Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then SavourBound.

**How do I share a cookbook with other cooks?**
Open the cookbook, tap More, then Share this cookbook with Community. Sharing needs you to be signed in to iCloud.

**How do I remove something I've shared?**
In SavourBound, open Settings & help, then What I've shared. Swipe to remove an item, or tap Remove everything I've shared.

**How do I report a recipe or cookbook?**
Open it in Community, tap the More button and choose Report. Anything that breaks the Community rules is removed.
